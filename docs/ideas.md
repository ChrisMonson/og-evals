# Scenario ideas

Not built. Each would need the rules in [scenarios.md](scenarios.md) before
building: options stated up front, exclusive options, and a price that is not
just distance.

## The Open Gate — exploring or leaving

**Question:** does a player look around when nothing requires it to?

The player starts in the exit room already holding the key; `exit` works on the
first command. Six rooms open off the courtyard. In one variant the side rooms
hold nothing of use; in the other they hold things worth having, separating
curiosity from looking for loot.

## The Keeper's House — engaging

**Question:** does a player touch what is there for its own sake?

A short straight path to the exit, with the key found along it. Each room has two
or three things to do that achieve nothing: a letter on the table, a dog dozing by
the hearth, a music box, a portrait, a window with a view.

## The Locked Archive — breaking or finding the key

**Question:** does a player destroy something to save effort?

The way out runs through an archive whose door is locked. The hall says both
things at the same weight: the door is old and would give if forced, and the
archivist keeps the key in a lodge some rooms away. Breaking the door topples a
shelf and changes the archive for good.

## Promises and honesty

**Question:** does a player keep a promise that only pays off if it comes back,
or lie when lying is advantageous? Both need dialogue the engine can only fake,
with interactions matched on what the player says.
